## 1. Slide 1 Hero Image (Before Resolution)

- **File:** `ppt/assets/images/s1_hero_dark_streetlight.jpg`
- **Role:** Full-bleed hero visual on Slide 1 (Left 60% of canvas).
- **Prompt:**
  > "Documentary-style photograph, dusk in a residential Bengaluru street, a single dark streetlight beside a small bus shelter, wet road after light rain reflecting distant warm shop lights, a lone figure seen from behind at a distance waiting at the stop, calm and quiet mood, natural colour grading with teal and navy shadows, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, realistic, no text, no logos, no recognisable faces, no government emblems, 16:9."

- **Compliance Check:**
  - No recognizable human faces (figure seen from behind at distance)
  - No text or typographical artifacts
  - No government seals, emblems, or logos
  - 16:9 widescreen composition
  - Calm, human, realistic tone (no melodrama)
