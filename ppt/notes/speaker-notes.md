# Speaker Notes: CivicPulse AI Round 1 Pitch Deck

Paced for a calm, measured delivery at ~125 words per minute. Each slide takes ~35 seconds, totaling exactly 2 minutes 55 seconds across the 5 slides.

---

## Slide 1: A streetlight, reported three times
*Target time: 35 seconds (~75 words)*

> "This is Asha. Every evening, she walks home from the bus stop in Bengaluru along an unlit residential street. Over two weeks, she reported the broken streetlight three separate times on the municipal portal. Each report became a disconnected ticket in an unranked queue of sixty complaints on the desk of Kavitha, the ward engineer.
>
> In five and a half months of 2025, Bengaluru recorded over 126,000 civic grievances. Streetlights alone made up over 31%, while road maintenance resolved only 58%. The problem isn't that citizens don't care, or that engineers don't work. The problem is that reports pile up blindly."

---

## Slide 2: Reports pile up. Nothing gets ranked.
*Target time: 35 seconds (~78 words)*

> "Today, municipal portals operate on a first-come, first-served queue. When an issue goes unresolved, citizens file it again and again. This artificially inflates backlogs. More dangerously, it blinds engineers: a critical safety hazard filed today sits behind a cosmetic repair filed last week. And because citizens never receive updates, trust collapses.
>
> CivicPulse changes this path completely. A citizen reports with free text and a map pin. Our AI understands the context, our Duplicate Radar surfaces existing reports, our deterministic engine scores the urgency, and officers dispatch targeted action."

---

## Slide 3: AI suggests. Code decides. People confirm.
*Target time: 36 seconds (~80 words)*

> "Trust requires complete explainability. We divide responsibilities strictly: AI suggests, code decides, people confirm.
>
> First, Google Gemini reads the unstructured text to identify category, safety flags, and a neutral summary. If the AI is ever offline, a deterministic keyword fallback takes over immediately.
>
> Second, Duplicate Radar compares coordinates and semantic embeddings within 500 meters and 30 days. Instead of filing duplicates, neighbors tap 'Add my voice'.
>
> Finally, our Priority Receipt calculates a transparent 0-to-100 score using seven auditable factors. Gemini has zero influence on this score. Ward engineers retain full authority to confirm or adjust."

## Slide 4: It works today
*Target time: 34 seconds (~75 words)*

>

In the center, ward engineers manage a ranked queue where urgent public hazards immediately rise to the top with SLA countdowns. On the right, live geospatial maps highlight ward clusters.
>
