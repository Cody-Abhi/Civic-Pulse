## 1. Primary Citations (Used on Slide 1 & Slide 5)

### Fact 1.1: BBMP Civic Grievances Volume (2025)
- **Claim:** 126,974 BBMP grievances filed across ~5.5 months of 2025 (Jan 1 – Jun 19, 2025) across 198 wards in Bengaluru (>750 complaints/day).
- **Exact Source:** OpenCity.in — *Decoding Bengaluru’s Civic Complaints 2025* (Published July 2025). Analysis of official Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Sahaaya / grievance portal records.
- **URL:** `https://opencity.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**

### Fact 1.2: Streetlight Complaint Concentration
- **Claim:** Streetlight failures accounted for 31.4% of total BBMP grievances (with overall electrical & street lighting issues accounting for 33.2%).
- **Exact Source:** OpenCity.in — *Decoding Bengaluru’s Civic Complaints 2025* (July 2025).
- **URL:** `https://opencity.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**

### Fact 1.3: Road Maintenance Resolution Rate Gap
- **Claim:** Road maintenance grievances lagged significantly behind other civic sectors, achieving a resolution rate of only 58% (compared to electrical at 96.4%).
- **Exact Source:** OpenCity.in — *Decoding Bengaluru’s Civic Complaints 2025* (July 2025).
- **URL:** `https://opencity.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**

## 2. Backup Citations

### Backup 2.1: National Road Safety & Pothole Fatalities (MoRTH)
- **Claim:** Potholes and related road surface defects claimed 2,161 lives in 2023 (a 16.4% year-on-year increase). Total road accident fatalities across India reached 172,890.
- **Exact Source:** Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India — *Road Accidents in India 2023* Report.
- **URL:** `https://morth.gov.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**

### Backup 2.2: Central Grievance Volume & Pendency (CPGRAMS / DARPG / PIB)
- **Claim:** CPGRAMS receives over 1.5–2 million public grievances annually, with average monthly Central Secretariat disposal exceeding 100,000 cases, and average disposal turnaround reduced to 12–13 days through digitized reforms.
- **Exact Source:** Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) / Press Information Bureau (PIB PRID 2203903, 2025–2026 Monthly CPGRAMS Reports).
- **URL:** `https://pib.gov.in`
- **Access Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
